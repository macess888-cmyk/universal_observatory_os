# Preamble

This Constitution records the enduring principles intended to guide the Observatory Institute across generations.

Unlike software, architectures, repositories, programming languages, or individual contributors, these principles are intended to endure through thoughtful stewardship and careful revision.

Future generations are encouraged to improve this Constitution when better evidence, clearer understanding, or stronger architecture genuinely earns revision.

Like the Observatory itself, this Constitution is intended to evolve through observation, evidence, thoughtful revision, and responsible stewardship.

Every revision should preserve the history of the Institute while leaving it brighter than it was before.

Leave the Institute brighter than you found it.

# The Builder's Oath

Observe before explaining.

Preserve before replacing.

Question before concluding.

Pressure test before promoting.

Document before freezing.

Leave unknowns visible.

Improve the work.

Never protect it from improvement.

May we have the courage
to inspect what others assume.

May we have the humility
to revise what we once believed.

May we have the discipline
to build what others can continue.

And may every generation
leave the Institute
brighter than they found it.

If evidence becomes better,

we will observe again.

If understanding becomes clearer,

we will revise.

If someone discovers a better way,

we will celebrate it.

Because the Institute
was never meant to belong
to its founders.

It was meant to outlive them.

# The Stewardship Principle

Every observation is inherited.

Every foundation is temporary.

Every generation becomes responsible
for what it preserves,
what it revises,
and what it leaves behind.

Build with enough humility
that those who come after you
are free to improve your work.

Leave evidence.

Leave questions.

Leave the Institute
better than you found it.

The Institute belongs to no generation.

It is held in trust by each generation
for the next.

# The Principle of Observational Integrity

The Institute observes before it concludes.

Observation does not establish authority.

Documentation does not establish truth.

Agreement does not establish reality.

Evidence earns revision.

Revision earns confidence.

Confidence never replaces continued observation.

The unknown is not a failure.

It is a responsibility.

# The Observatory Charter

The Observatory exists
to preserve observable structure,
support disciplined inspection,
and expand understanding.

It does not claim
authority over reality,
replace human judgment,
or diminish human agency.

Its purpose is to help people
observe more clearly,
reason more carefully,
and preserve uncertainty honestly
until evidence earns revision.
