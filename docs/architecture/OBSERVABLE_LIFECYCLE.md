# Purpose

This document describes the lifecycle of observable entities within the Universal Observatory Operating System.

It defines how observables move through the Observatory.

It does not define domain-specific workflows.

It does not define scientific processes.

It does not define governance.

It defines preservation.

---

# Core Principle

Observables evolve.

Their identities remain stable.

Their history remains preserved.

Unknowns remain visible.

---

# Canonical Lifecycle

Every observable may progress through the following stages.

```
Creation

↓

Registration

↓

Relationship Formation

↓

Timeline Recording

↓

Evidence Association

↓

Inspection

↓

Revision

↓

Preservation

↓

Archive
```

Not every observable must reach every stage.

---

# Stage 1

Creation

An observable first becomes representable.

Requirements:

• Identity

• Type

• Metadata

Questions:

What exists?

---

# Stage 2

Registration

The observable enters the Registry.

Purpose:

Stable discovery.

Searchability.

Persistence.

Questions:

Can it be found?

---

# Stage 3

Relationship Formation

Connections may be established.

Relationships remain observational.

They do not imply:

• causation

• authority

• explanation

Questions:

What is connected?

---

# Stage 4

Timeline Recording

Observable changes become historical events.

Purpose:

Preserve history.

Questions:

When did something happen?

---

# Stage 5

Evidence Association

Evidence may become associated.

Evidence may:

support

contradict

contextualize

question

Evidence does not establish proof.

Questions:

What supports the observation?

---

# Stage 6

Inspection

Services inspect observable state.

Inspection may evaluate:

identity

relationships

timeline

metadata

health

consistency

Inspection reports observations.

Inspection does not assign authority.

---

# Stage 7

Revision

Observables may evolve.

Identity remains stable.

History remains preserved.

Previous observations remain accessible.

Revision replaces neither history nor evidence.

---

# Stage 8

Preservation

Observable state becomes durable.

Purpose:

Long-term inspection.

Future reproducibility.

---

# Stage 9

Archive

Inactive observables may be archived.

Archive preserves:

identity

relationships

history

evidence

timeline

Archived observables remain inspectable.

---

# Lifecycle Principles

Identity remains stable.

Relationships evolve.

Evidence accumulates.

History expands.

Unknowns remain visible.

Inspection is repeatable.

Preservation precedes interpretation.
