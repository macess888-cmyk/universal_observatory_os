# Purpose

This milestone records the beginning of the Explorer Era.

Previous eras established the Observatory's principles,
architecture,
foundation,
runtime,
and capability composition.

The Explorer Era begins the transition from building
the Observatory itself to creating experiences through which
people can inspect, navigate, and understand observable structure.

The Observatory is now capable of presenting its own work
to human observers.

---

# Explorer Foundation

The Explorer introduces the first human-centered capability.

Its purpose is to help people:

- inspect
- navigate
- compare
- explore
- preserve uncertainty
- identify future inspection targets

The Explorer introduces no new authority.

It presents observable structure.

# Human Arrival

The Explorer marks the first architectural layer intended primarily
for the Observatory's users rather than its internal runtime.

This represents a significant architectural transition.

The Observatory Runtime now serves human exploration.
